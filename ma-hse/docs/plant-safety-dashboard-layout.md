# Plant safety dashboard

The plant dashboard opens with a compact overview of whole-plant indicators and a single safety-days counter. The overview retains the existing permissions and metric definitions: accidents with injury are not relabelled as lost-time-only accidents, and rates without worked hours remain not applicable. SIF/PSIF displays counts and compares them with counts from the homologous period.

Period controls are expandable. Changing department submits immediately and retains the selected year/month or exact date range. N2 and N4 continue to default to their assigned department. Selecting all departments includes unassigned communications.

The department section contains the communication pyramid and one primary ranking at a time. The other employee, department and workstation rankings remain available under More rankings. The existing chart studio is available under Explore charts and retains its restrictions on departmental metrics.

The pyramid shows its total once, separates counts from percentages and identifies validated and pending communications. Selecting a layer opens its scoped records and links to their communication pages. The list initially shows 20 records and supports showing more. Native dialog behavior supports keyboard focus and Escape. The homologous comparison remains available in an expandable section. Unclassified accidents retain the existing counting behavior and are not assigned a severity by the layout.

All previous detailed KPI groups remain available in an expandable whole-plant section. The corporate dashboard retains its existing presentation; compact rankings are explicitly enabled only by the plant page.

Validation includes component interactions, department integration tests, and Chromium layout checks from 320px to 1920px with KPI details both collapsed and expanded.
